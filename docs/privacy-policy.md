# BalanceLoop — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 21 August 2026**

BalanceLoop ("the app") is operated by Logarithm Studio. This policy explains what
the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to get it deleted.

Contact: **contact@logarithmstudio.com**

---

## What we collect

**Account details** — your email address, username, display name, and profile photo
if you upload one. Provided by you at sign-up, or by Google if you sign in with Google.

**Your shared-expense records** — expenses, amounts, descriptions, categories, who paid,
how a bill was split, balances between you and others, settlements you record, and the
groups you belong to. This is the core function of the app.

**Contacts you add manually** — when you add someone who does not use BalanceLoop (a
"local contact"), we store the name and username you give them so expenses can be split
with them. Please only add details you are entitled to share.

**Device and technical data** — a push notification token so we can notify you, and
whether your device is online, so the app can work offline and sync later. A copy of your
own data is cached on your device so it stays readable without a connection.

**Crash reports** — if the app crashes we receive a technical report. It is configured
not to attach personal identifiers or IP addresses.

## What we do NOT collect

- **Payment or card details.** BalanceLoop only *records* who paid whom. It never
  processes, holds or transfers money, and is not connected to any payment provider.
- Your device's address book or phone contacts.
- Your location.
- Advertising identifiers. There is no advertising in the app, and no third-party
  analytics or tracking provider is connected.

## Who your data is shared with

**Other users, by design.** An expense is a shared record. People you split with can see
the expense, the amount, the split, and your display name and photo. Group members can
see each other. This is inherent to how the app works.

We use these service providers to run the app:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Supabase | Account authentication and database hosting |
| Cloudinary | Storing profile and group images |
| Resend (via Amazon SES) | Sending account emails such as password resets |
| Expo & Google Firebase Cloud Messaging | Delivering push notifications |
| Sentry | Crash and error reports |
| Google | Optional "Sign in with Google" |

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

## How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account is active. After deletion, see below.

## Deleting your account

In the app: **Profile → Login & Security → Danger Zone → Delete Account**.

If you cannot access the app, email **contact@logarithmstudio.com** from your registered
address and we will action the deletion for you.

**You must settle your outstanding balances first.** Deletion is blocked while you owe,
or are owed, money by another user. This is deliberate: a balance is a shared record, and
removing your side of it while the other person is still relying on it would leave their
account inaccurate. Settle up, or ask the other person to record the settlement, and the
option becomes available.

### What is permanently erased

- Your sign-in credentials — the account can never be accessed again
- Your email address, username, display name and profile photo
- Your push notification tokens and notification settings
- Your activity feed, friend connections, and any pending requests

### What is kept, anonymised

Expenses and balances you shared with other people are **kept**, with your name replaced
by "Deleted user".

This is because those records are not only yours. If you and a friend split a bill, that
expense is equally their record of what happened, and erasing it would silently change
their history and their balances with other people. Once anonymised the data no longer
identifies you.

Groups you created are transferred to another member rather than deleted, so the other
members do not lose their shared history.

## Your rights

You can access and correct your account details in the app at any time, and delete your
account as described above. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights
over your personal data — contact us at the address above and we will respond.

## Children

BalanceLoop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their
data.
